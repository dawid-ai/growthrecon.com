the methodology

The System

Five stages, in order. Each builds on the last. Research reveals truth. Expose filters what matters. Convert builds on that truth. Optimize systematizes it. Navigate makes it stick.

R 01 / research

Research

Map the business through the customer's eyes. ICP, language, pricing, data - the foundation everything else is built on.

Output: The Source Doc
E 02 / expose

Expose

Strip away vanity metrics. Find the ONE objective. Show where money's actually going and what's actually working.

Output: The Kill List
C 03 / convert

Convert

Fix every leak from first touch to lifetime value. Foundation before traffic - you can't test what's broken.

Output: Working Funnel
O 04 / optimize

Optimize

Build trackable processes. Wire every action to the goal. Then reapply the framework to catch what you missed.

Output: Operating System
N 05 / navigate

Navigate

Drive adoption through internal resistance. Train, hand over, and leave behind a self-running system.
